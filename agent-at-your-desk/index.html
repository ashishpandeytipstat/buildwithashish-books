Four ideas from inside

Real pages, not a pitch deck

Every chapter is built around a diagram you can use the same day. Here are four of them, straight from the book.

The REPEAT check

Should this job go to an agent at all?

Score Repeats, Explainable, Pipes exist, Errors cheap, Approvable and Time-hungry from 0 to 2. 9 to 12 means delegate now; 0 to 5 means keep it or redesign it first.

Pixels vs pipes

One tool call beats twenty clicks

Screenshots and clicks are slow and fragile. A tool call through the Model Context Protocol is fast and easy to check. Use pipes wherever they exist, and pixels only for the last mile.

The five-minute review

Don't watch it work. Check it.

Read the summary and confidence list, spot-check three items, verify one number at its source, open it the way the recipient will, then approve. If two of three spot-checks fail, fix the brief and rerun.
